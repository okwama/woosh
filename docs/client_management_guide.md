# Client Management System Documentation

## Overview

The Woosh Client Management System provides comprehensive customer relationship management (CRM) capabilities designed specifically for field sales teams. The system enables sales representatives to maintain detailed client profiles, track interactions, manage client stock levels, process payments, and analyze client performance to build stronger customer relationships and drive sales growth.

## 👥 Core Features

### Client Database Management
- **Comprehensive Client Profiles** - Detailed customer information and contact management
- **Client Categorization** - Organize clients by type, priority, and business status
- **Contact History** - Complete interaction timeline and communication records
- **Document Management** - Store contracts, agreements, and important documents

### Client Interactions
- **Visit History** - Detailed records of all client visits and outcomes
- **Communication Logs** - Track calls, emails, and messages
- **Follow-up Management** - Automated reminders for scheduled follow-ups
- **Feedback Collection** - Systematic client feedback and satisfaction tracking

### Client Stock Management
- **Inventory Tracking** - Monitor client's product stock levels
- **Reorder Alerts** - Notifications when client stock runs low
- **Stock Recommendations** - Suggest optimal inventory levels
- **Supply Chain Integration** - Coordinate client restocking needs

### Payment Processing
- **Payment Collection** - Process various payment methods
- **Payment History** - Complete transaction records and receipts
- **Outstanding Balances** - Track pending payments and credit limits
- **Financial Reporting** - Client-specific financial analytics

## 🚀 User Flows

### 1. Client Registration and Onboarding

#### Flow Overview
```
Lead Identification → Initial Contact → Client Information Collection → Verification → Profile Creation → Onboarding → First Order
```

#### Step-by-Step Process

**Step 1: Lead Identification**
- Identify potential clients through:
  - Referrals from existing clients
  - Cold calling and prospecting
  - Marketing campaign responses
  - Trade show contacts
  - Online inquiries
- Capture basic lead information:
  - Business name and contact person
  - Phone number and email
  - Business type and location
  - Initial interest level

**Step 2: Initial Client Contact**
- Schedule initial meeting or call
- Conduct needs assessment:
  - Business requirements
  - Current suppliers and products
  - Budget and volume expectations
  - Decision-making process
- Document conversation outcomes
- Assess qualification criteria

**Step 3: Client Information Collection**
- Gather comprehensive business details:
  - **Business Information**
    - Legal business name
    - Trading name (if different)
    - Business registration number
    - Tax identification number
    - Industry classification
  - **Contact Information**
    - Primary contact person and title
    - Phone numbers (office, mobile)
    - Email addresses
    - Physical address
    - Billing address (if different)
  - **Business Details**
    - Years in operation
    - Number of employees
    - Annual revenue (estimated)
    - Current suppliers
    - Product needs and preferences

**Step 4: Client Verification**
- Verify business credentials:
  - Business registration validation
  - Credit check (if required)
  - Reference verification
  - Bank details confirmation
- Risk assessment:
  - Credit limit determination
  - Payment terms evaluation
  - Business stability analysis

**Step 5: Profile Creation**
- Create comprehensive client profile in system
- Set up client categories and tags:
  - Client type (Retailer, Distributor, End User)
  - Priority level (VIP, High, Medium, Low)
  - Business size (Large, Medium, Small)
  - Geographic region
- Configure client-specific settings:
  - Credit limits and payment terms
  - Pricing agreements
  - Delivery preferences
  - Communication preferences

**Step 6: Client Onboarding**
- Welcome communication and introduction
- Provide company information and catalogs
- Set up initial product demonstrations
- Establish communication protocols
- Plan first visit and initial order

### 3. Client Interaction Management

#### Flow Overview
```
Plan Interaction → Execute Interaction → Document Outcome → Schedule Follow-up → Update Client Status
```

#### Step-by-Step Process

**Step 1: Interaction Planning**
- Schedule client interactions:
  - **Visit Planning**
    - Select visit type (Sales, Service, Follow-up)
    - Set objectives and agenda
    - Prepare materials and presentations
    - Book appointment with client
  - **Call Planning**
    - Define call purpose and goals
    - Prepare talking points
    - Schedule convenient time
    - Set expected duration

**Step 2: Interaction Execution**
- **During Client Visits**
  - Check in at client location (GPS verified)
  - Access client history and previous notes
  - Conduct planned activities:
    - Product presentations
    - Order discussions
    - Relationship building
    - Issue resolution
  - Take notes and photos as appropriate
  - Collect client feedback and concerns

- **During Phone Calls**
  - Access client profile during call
  - Document conversation points
  - Record outcomes and decisions
  - Note any action items or commitments

**Step 3: Interaction Documentation**
- Complete interaction summary:
  - **Visit/Call Details**
    - Date, time, and duration
    - Participants involved
    - Topics discussed
    - Outcomes achieved
  - **Business Outcomes**
    - Orders placed or discussed
    - Opportunities identified
    - Issues raised and resolved
    - Client feedback received
  - **Follow-up Actions**
    - Commitments made
    - Information to provide
    - Next steps planned
    - Follow-up timeline

**Step 4: Follow-up Management**
- Schedule follow-up activities:
  - Set reminders for commitments
  - Plan next visit or call
  - Assign action items to team members
  - Update client status and notes
- Automated follow-up triggers:
  - Pending quotation follow-up
  - Post-delivery check-in
  - Payment reminder calls
  - Relationship maintenance visits

### 4. Client Stock Management

#### Flow Overview
```
Monitor Stock Levels → Analyze Usage Patterns → Generate Recommendations → Coordinate Restocking → Track Delivery
```

#### Step-by-Step Process

**Step 1: Stock Level Monitoring**
- Track client inventory in real-time:
  - Current stock quantities
  - Product categories and variants
  - Stock movement history
  - Consumption patterns
- Set up monitoring alerts:
  - Low stock warnings
  - Reorder point notifications
  - Fast-moving item alerts
  - Slow-moving stock identification

**Step 2: Usage Analysis**
- Analyze consumption patterns:
  - **Historical Data Analysis**
    - Monthly/quarterly usage trends
    - Seasonal variations
    - Product category performance
    - Peak consumption periods
  - **Predictive Analytics**
    - Forecast future needs
    - Identify reorder timing
    - Suggest optimal stock levels
    - Recommend new products

**Step 3: Stock Recommendations**
- Generate intelligent recommendations:
  - **Reorder Suggestions**
    - Optimal order quantities
    - Recommended products
    - Timing for next order
    - Budget considerations
  - **Inventory Optimization**
    - Suggest stock level adjustments
    - Recommend product mix changes
    - Identify overstock situations
    - Propose clearance strategies

**Step 4: Restocking Coordination**
- Facilitate client restocking:
  - **Order Creation**
    - Pre-populate orders with recommendations
    - Adjust quantities based on client input
    - Apply appropriate pricing and terms
    - Process order confirmation
  - **Delivery Coordination**
    - Schedule delivery timing
    - Coordinate logistics
    - Track shipment status
    - Confirm delivery completion

### 5. Payment Processing

#### Flow Overview
```
Payment Request → Payment Method Selection → Transaction Processing → Receipt Generation → Record Update
```

#### Step-by-Step Process

**Step 1: Payment Initiation**
- Identify payment opportunities:
  - Outstanding invoice collections
  - Cash on delivery payments
  - Advance payments for orders
  - Settlement of outstanding balances
- Calculate payment amounts:
  - Principal amount due
  - Applicable taxes
  - Discounts or adjustments
  - Total amount to collect

**Step 2: Payment Method Selection**
- Support multiple payment methods:
  - **Cash Payments**
    - Count and verify cash
    - Provide immediate receipt
    - Record denominations received
  - **Bank Transfers**
    - Provide bank account details
    - Generate payment reference
    - Track transfer status
  - **Digital Payments**
    - Mobile money transfers
    - Card payments (if equipped)
    - Online banking payments
  - **Cheques**
    - Verify cheque details
    - Note clearing timeframes
    - Track collection status

**Step 3: Transaction Processing**
- Process payment transaction:
  - Validate payment amount
  - Apply any applicable discounts
  - Calculate change (for cash payments)
  - Generate transaction reference
  - Update client balance immediately

**Step 4: Receipt and Documentation**
- Generate comprehensive receipt:
  - **Receipt Details**
    - Transaction date and time
    - Payment amount and method
    - Applied to specific invoices
    - Running balance after payment
  - **Digital Receipt**
    - Email or SMS receipt option
    - PDF format for records
    - Branding and company details
  - **Physical Receipt**
    - Printed copy for client
    - Carbon copy for records
    - Sequential numbering

**Step 5: Financial Record Updates**
- Update all related financial records:
  - Client account balance
  - Outstanding invoice status
  - Payment history log
  - Financial reporting data
  - Commission calculations
